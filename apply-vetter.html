Become a Vetter

Manarah relies on qualified teachers and imams to vouch for the khateebs on our platform. If you teach students of knowledge and are willing to review and endorse those in your care, we need you.

What vetters do

Review applications

When a student you teach applies to join Manarah, we'll reach out to you to confirm their credentials and character.

Provide a testimonial

A short quote from you appears on the khateeb's profile page, giving communities confidence in who they're inviting.

Stay informed

You'll be notified when a student you've endorsed receives bookings, and can flag any concerns at any time.

Who we're looking for

  • Qualified scholar, ustadh, or imam with recognized credentials
  • Currently teaching or mentoring students of Islamic knowledge
  • Willing to be contacted for reference checks
  • Based in Canada or familiar with the Canadian Muslim community context
