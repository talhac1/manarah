How it works

Three simple steps from request to khutbah, bi'idhnillāh.

i.

Browse & choose

Read each khateeb's bio, watch past khutbahs, and review testimonials from their teachers and the communities they've served.

ii.

Submit a request

Share your jumu'ah date, location, audience, and any topic preferences. Most khateebs respond within 24 hours, in shā' Allāh.

iii.

Confirm & host

Once availability is confirmed, you'll receive a written agreement, contact details, and any travel logistics needed for the day.
