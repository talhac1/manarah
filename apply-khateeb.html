Apply as a Khateeb

We're looking for sincere, knowledgeable student khateebs to join Manarah. If you've delivered Jumu'ah khutbahs and are vouched for by a teacher, we'd love to hear from you.

Who we're looking for

  • Currently studying Islamic knowledge under a qualified teacher
  • Has delivered at least 3 Jumu'ah khutbahs
  • Can provide a teacher reference who will vouch for you
  • Committed to punctuality and clear communication
  • Based in Canada (currently Ontario-focused)

What happens next

We'll review your application and reach out to your teacher reference. If everything checks out, you'll receive a profile on Manarah within 1–2 weeks, in shā' Allāh.

Your profile will be reviewed and updated with you before it goes live.

Questions?

Reach us at salam@manarah.org
