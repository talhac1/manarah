About the khateeb

Bio

Br. Idris Mohammed is a youth worker and student of aqeedah and fiqh, completing his B.A. in Religious Studies at Wilfrid Laurier University.

He studies under Sh. Yasir Qadhi and Ustadh Bilal Saqib at AlMaghrib Institute, bringing the rigor of classical Islamic scholarship to the lived realities of Muslim youth in Canada.

For the past three years, Br. Idris has worked closely with high school MSAs in Waterloo Region — running weekly halaqahs, organizing youth retreats, and delivering khutbahs that speak directly to the lived realities of young Muslims in public school environments.
