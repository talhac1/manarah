About the khateeb

Bio

Br. Yusuf Rahman is a hifdh graduate with an ijaazah in the qira'ah of Hafs ʿan ʿĀsim, currently pursuing his Master's in Islamic Studies at the University of Toronto.

He studies under Sh. Hamid Slimi and Sh. Yusuf Badat, and brings the depth of a scholar alongside the warmth of a community mentor — accessible to students, grounded in scholarship.

Br. Yusuf has delivered over thirty Jumu'ah khutbahs across Toronto-area mosques and university campuses. His khutbahs blend the recitation of a hāfidh with the warmth of a mentor — accessible to students, grounded in scholarship.
