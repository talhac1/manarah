About the khateeb

Bio

Br. Zayaan Backus is a student that strives to seek Islamic knowledge with a passion for Qur'an and fiqh. He has memorized one manzil of the Qur'an and continues his hifdh alongside his university studies.

He studies under Ustadh Syed Hassan and Ustadh Danyal Khan at Taqwa Institute, and works with Roots Academy as a youth mentor and program facilitator — bringing classical Islamic learning into a curriculum that meets young Muslims where they are.

A pillar in the McMaster University community, he has led halaqahs, Qur'an circles, and served as McMaster MSA Treasurer — bringing both knowledge and lived student experience to the manarah. His khutbahs are known for clarity, sincerity, and a deep grounding in the texts of the Qur'an and Sunnah.
